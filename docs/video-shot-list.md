# Combat Gym: demo video shot list (Boxing Levels 1-2)

14 short clips. Each one shows the move done correctly, slowly, then at normal speed.

## Filming rules (all clips)

- Length: 10-20 seconds. Loops in the app, so start and end in the same stance.
- Format: landscape 16:9, 720p or 1080p, MP4. No music needed (the app plays muted).
- Angle: front view first, then side view (or two separate takes).
- Setting: plain wall, good light from the front, full body in frame (head to feet).
- Clothing: fitted, contrasting with the wall, so arms and feet are easy to see.
- Speed: 2 reps slow, 2 reps normal speed. Return to guard every time.
- Stance: orthodox (left foot forward). The app tells southpaws to mirror.
- No gloves needed for Level 1-2. Hand wraps are fine.
- Every clip must be checked by the reviewing coach before it goes in the app.
